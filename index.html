SDG 7 — Affordable and Clean Energy

By shifting flexible fleet charging into overnight low-tariff periods, VoltWise shaves peak demand and prevents unnecessary transformer overload. This doubles the utilization efficiency of existing electrical distribution assets without requiring carbon-heavy substation expansions.

SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities

Electrifying municipal and logistics fleets is vital for urban air quality. VoltWise ensures commercial delivery carts, shuttle vans, and utility vehicles reliably reach their required state of charge by morning departure deadlines, supporting dependable zero-emission city transit.

1. Mathematical & Engineering Equations
• Battery Energy Required: E_batt = BatteryCapacity × max(0, TargetSOC - CurrentSOC) / 100
• Grid Energy Consumed: E_grid = E_batt / η (where efficiency η = 0.90)
• Time Slot Energy (15 min = 0.25 h): E_slot = Power_kW × 0.25 h
• Total Electricity Cost = ∑ (E_slot,grid × Tariff_t)
• Urgency Slack: Slack(t) = SlotsRemaining(t) - SlotsNeededAtFullPower
2. Judge Defense Bank & Frequently Asked Questions
Q: Why not use Machine Learning or an AI neural network for the scheduler?
A: Mission-critical electrical grid engineering demands deterministic guarantees. Neural networks can hallucinate or produce probabilistic constraint violations that trip physical circuit breakers. Our deterministic greedy heuristic provides 100% reproducible results, runs in milliseconds offline in the browser, and is fully explainable to depot engineers.
Q: How do you prevent two vehicles from using the same charger?
A: In each time slot, chargers are tracked in a set and assigned exclusively. Our independent constraint validator scans all 48 slots and asserts charger exclusivity, vehicle exclusivity, and site limits.
Q: How is the baseline defined?
A: Our baseline represents uncoordinated ASAP (As Soon As Possible) charging—the default real-world approach where vehicles plug in and pull maximum power immediately on arrival, ignoring tariffs.
